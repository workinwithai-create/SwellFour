# SwellFour

Four-bar live string-and-horn swell desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop DAWs slam the hook with no air under it. Session players write the last four bars of the pre as a live swell: violin climb, cello floor, horn stack, hairpin, common-tone hold, late brass, hush then bloom, octave lift, pedal under, last-bar hit. SwellFour seats those chairs, plays pocket vs swell, and exports a punch list.

Live samples only — FluidR3 violin, cello (upright-bass chair), french horn (trumpet chair), acoustic grand, nylon, kit from the PreEight sample CDN. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

Today already shipped ChoirEight (choir loft) and SlideTwo (808 slide). SwellFour is the missing orchestral hairpin: not a choir, not a bass slide, not an FX lift.

X signal this week: producers get ghosted, mix themselves into writer's block, and abandon tracks after ear fatigue. The swell is the cheapest way to make an unfinished hook feel finished without rewriting the song.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Pre, Porch Rise, Fold Radio, Stair House, Carbon Verse).
2. Hear A: the pocket. Four bars of the current pre. It loops.
3. Stamp a swell recipe.
4. Hear B: four of pocket, four of swell. Then it stops. The hook would start on 9.
5. Copy the punch list. Drop the idea on the last four of the pre.
